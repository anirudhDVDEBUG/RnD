# Build Ship Grow — Content & Growth Strategy Skill

A structured skill for planning and executing content creation and growth strategies for digital products, following the Build → Ship → Grow methodology.

## When to use

- "Help me create a content strategy for my product launch"
- "Build a growth plan to acquire my first 1000 users"
- "Generate a launch checklist for shipping my side project"
- "Plan a content calendar focused on audience building"
- "Create a distribution strategy for my new feature release"

## How to use

### 1. Define the Product or Project

Start by providing context about what you're building:
- Product name and one-line description
- Target audience and their primary pain points
- Current stage: **Build** (pre-launch), **Ship** (launching), or **Grow** (post-launch)
- Key differentiators and value proposition

### 2. Build Phase — Content Foundation

When in the Build phase, the skill helps you:
- Draft a compelling README and landing page copy
- Create "building in public" content (dev logs, progress updates)
- Identify 3–5 content pillars aligned with your audience's interests
- Outline an initial content calendar (2–4 weeks)

### 3. Ship Phase — Launch Content

When ready to ship, the skill generates:
- Launch announcement posts (Twitter/X thread, LinkedIn, blog)
- Product Hunt or Hacker News submission copy
- Email sequences for early adopters
- A launch-day checklist with timing and channel assignments

### 4. Grow Phase — Sustained Growth Strategy

After launch, the skill helps you plan:
- Weekly content cadence across channels (blog, social, newsletter)
- SEO-optimized article outlines targeting relevant keywords
- Community engagement tactics and feedback loops
- Growth metric tracking (what to measure, how to iterate)
- Repurposing strategy: turn one piece of content into 5+ formats

### 5. Review and Iterate

- Audit existing content for gaps and opportunities
- A/B test messaging and headlines
- Adjust strategy based on what channels drive the most traction

## Output Formats

The skill can produce:
- **Content calendars** in Markdown table format
- **Launch checklists** as actionable task lists
- **Growth plans** as structured documents with phases and milestones
- **Social media copy** ready to post
- **Blog outlines** with SEO considerations
