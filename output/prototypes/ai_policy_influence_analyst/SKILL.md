# AI Policy Influence Analyst

Analyze how AI companies shape policy, ethics frameworks, and institutional narratives to align with their commercial interests.

## When to use

- "How is [company] influencing AI regulation?"
- "Analyze the lobbying strategy behind this AI policy document"
- "What vendor interests are embedded in this AI ethics framework?"
- "Compare how AI companies position their technical limitations as ethical principles"
- "Identify corporate influence in this AI governance proposal"

## How to use

1. **Identify the policy artifact**: Gather the regulation, framework, encyclical, or governance document being analyzed.

2. **Map vendor interests**: For each principle or constraint in the document, ask:
   - Which company's technical architecture does this favor?
   - Does this enshrine a specific vendor's limitation as a universal principle?
   - Who benefits commercially from this framing?

3. **Trace influence channels**: Document the path from vendor to policy:
   - Direct lobbying and testimony
   - Advisory board placements
   - Academic funding and research partnerships
   - Relationships with policymakers and institutional leaders
   - Published safety research that frames the discourse

4. **Assess the framing technique**: Categorize the strategy:
   - **Limitation laundering**: Reframing technical constraints as ethical choices
   - **Safety capture**: Defining "safe AI" to match one's own architecture
   - **Standards setting**: Getting your approach codified before competitors catch up
   - **Institutional blessing**: Securing endorsement from trusted authorities (religious, academic, governmental)

5. **Produce analysis**: Write a structured brief covering:
   - The policy/framework being influenced
   - The influencing parties and their commercial interests
   - The specific technical positions being elevated to ethical principles
   - The effectiveness and precedent of the lobbying approach

## Context

As Corey Quinn observed regarding Anthropic co-founder Christopher Olah's influence on the papal encyclical *Magnifica Humanitas*: "Getting the literal Pope to canonize your product's specific technical limitations as a spiritual treatise is the single greatest act of vendor lobbying I have ever seen."

This illustrates how AI companies can achieve regulatory and institutional capture by positioning their technical choices as universal ethical imperatives.
