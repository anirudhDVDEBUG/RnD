# Authentic Founder Voice

A skill that strips AI-generated polish from founder communications and replaces it with genuine, direct human voice. Inspired by Paul Graham's observation that AI-written founder emails are immediately recognizable and actively harmful to credibility.

## When to use

- "Write an email to an investor about our progress"
- "Help me draft a cold outreach to Paul Graham / a VC / an advisor"
- "Make this founder update sound more natural"
- "Review my pitch email — does it sound AI-generated?"
- "Rewrite this so it doesn't sound like ChatGPT wrote it"

## How to use

### Step 1: Identify AI-voice red flags

Scan the draft (or your own output) for these giveaways:
- **Hard-hitting journalistic style** — no founder naturally writes this way
- Unnecessary superlatives ("revolutionary", "groundbreaking", "game-changing")
- Overly parallel sentence structures and listicle formatting
- Corporate filler ("I hope this message finds you well", "I wanted to reach out")
- Perfectly balanced paragraphs with topic sentences
- Em dashes and semicolons used for rhetorical flourish
- Phrases like "at the intersection of", "leveraging", "synergies"

### Step 2: Rewrite in authentic founder voice

Apply these principles:

1. **Be direct.** Say what you want in the first sentence. "We built X. It does Y. We want Z from you."
2. **Be specific.** Replace polished generalities with concrete numbers, dates, and facts.
3. **Be imperfect.** Real emails are slightly rough. Short sentences. Occasional fragments. That's fine.
4. **Show your thinking, not your rhetoric.** Explain *why* something matters to you personally, not why it should matter to them.
5. **Keep it short.** Founders are busy. Investors are busier. Three short paragraphs max for cold outreach.
6. **Write like you talk.** Read it aloud. If you wouldn't say it in conversation, cut it.

### Step 3: Apply the Paul Graham test

Before finalizing, ask:
- Would the reader suspect AI wrote this? If yes, rewrite.
- Does it sound like every other pitch email? If yes, add something only *this* founder would say.
- Is there anything here that "feels like being lied to"? If yes, remove it.

### Example transformation

**AI-polished (bad):**
> Dear Mr. Graham, I hope this finds you well. I'm the co-founder of DataSync, a revolutionary platform at the intersection of real-time analytics and enterprise collaboration. Our groundbreaking approach leverages cutting-edge ML to deliver unprecedented insights...

**Authentic founder voice (good):**
> Hi — I'm building DataSync. We pipe live database changes into team dashboards. 400 companies use it, mostly found us through word of mouth. We're applying to YC because we have more demand than we can handle and need help figuring out pricing.

### Key reminder

As Paul Graham noted: "It's not impressive to use AI to write stuff for you; any teenager can do that." The value is in *your* authentic perspective. Use AI to clarify your thinking, not to replace your voice.
