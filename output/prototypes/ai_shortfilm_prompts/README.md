# AI Short Film Prompts

**Generate structured, model-optimized video prompts for AI short films.**
Based on the methodology behind *Zombie Scavenger* by Mx-Shell — called "one of the best short films I've seen in years" by PJ Ace.

```
$ python3 generate_prompts.py --model sora --format prompts

[Shot 1.1]
Extreme wide shot, slow crane up. A lone figure walks along a cracked
coastal highway in an abandoned city skyline half-submerged in rising
seawater... photorealistic, physically accurate, natural camera motion, 4K.
```

**10 shots across 4 scenes, ~90 seconds of cinematic AI video — generated in under a second.**
