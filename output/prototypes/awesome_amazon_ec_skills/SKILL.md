# Awesome Amazon EC Skills

A curated collection of Claude Code / AI Agent skills for Amazon cross-border e-commerce sellers (亚马逊跨境电商), with a focus on Amazon global selling and 1688 upstream sourcing.

## When to use

- "Help me optimize my Amazon product listing for the US marketplace"
- "Analyze competitor keywords and PPC strategy on Amazon"
- "Find suppliers on 1688 for my Amazon FBA product"
- "Generate a cross-border ecommerce sourcing and selling workflow"
- "Help me with Amazon SP-API integration for seller analytics"

## How to use

1. **Amazon Listing Optimization**: Ask Claude to review and optimize your product title, bullet points, description, and backend keywords for Amazon SEO. Provide your ASIN or product details for targeted suggestions.

2. **Keyword & Competitor Research**: Request competitor analysis, keyword mining, or PPC campaign recommendations. Claude can help structure SP-API queries to pull search term reports and advertising data.

3. **1688 Sourcing Integration**: Describe your product requirements and ask Claude to help draft 1688 supplier search criteria, compare quotes, or build a sourcing evaluation checklist for cross-border fulfillment (FBA/FBM).

4. **Review & Market Analytics**: Ask Claude to analyze review sentiment, identify product improvement opportunities, or assess market demand trends using available data.

5. **MCP Server Setup**: For automated workflows, reference the repo's MCP server configurations to connect Claude with Amazon SP-API endpoints for real-time seller data access.
