## Key Facts

From the **SpaceX S-1 filing** (SEC Archives, May 2026):

| Detail | Value |
|---|---|
| **Parties** | xAI (via SpaceX S-1 disclosure) and Anthropic PBC |
| **Agreement type** | Cloud Services Agreements |
| **Compute facilities** | COLOSSUS and COLOSSUS II |
| **Monthly fee** | $1.25 billion/month |
| **Term** | Through May 2029 (3 years) |
| **Total implied value** | ~$45 billion (36 months x $1.25B) |
| **Ramp period** | May-June 2026 at a reduced fee |
| **Termination clause** | Either party upon 90 days' notice |
| **Signing date** | May 2026 |

## How to use

1. **Summarize the deal**: Explain that Anthropic signed a Cloud Services Agreement with xAI to access compute capacity across the COLOSSUS and COLOSSUS II data centers, paying $1.25B/month through May 2029.

2. **Provide context on scale**: At $1.25B/month, this is one of the largest known cloud compute contracts in the AI industry. The ~$45B total value (before ramp discounts) dwarfs typical cloud commitments.

3. **Explain COLOSSUS**: xAI's COLOSSUS is a massive GPU cluster originally built to train Grok models. COLOSSUS II is the expanded successor. xAI is monetizing spare capacity by offering it to third-party customers like Anthropic.

4. **Note the termination flexibility**: The 90-day termination clause by either party provides significant flexibility compared to typical multi-year cloud commitments, suggesting this may function more like a capacity reservation than a locked-in contract.

5. **Discuss strategic implications**: Anthropic diversifying compute sources beyond traditional cloud providers (AWS, GCP) signals the extreme demand for GPU capacity among frontier AI labs and the emergence of AI companies as cloud infrastructure providers.

## Analysis Points

- **Anthropic's largest disclosed infrastructure commitment** — signals aggressive scaling of training and inference capacity.
- **xAI as cloud provider** — a new competitive dynamic where AI labs sell excess compute to rivals.
- **Comparison to AWS partnership** — Anthropic has a deep partnership with Amazon/AWS; this COLOSSUS deal suggests AWS capacity alone is insufficient for Anthropic's needs.
- **COLOSSUS II dual use** — xAI trains Grok 5 on COLOSSUS II while simultaneously leasing capacity to Anthropic, indicating massive total cluster size.
