# Build DNA — Business Ideation from Your Unique Strengths

A skill that transforms your personal skills, experiences, and unfair advantages into actionable business ideas. Through adaptive intake, it generates personalized ideas only you could build — complete with market analysis, go-to-market plan, MVP roadmap, and a 72-hour action plan to your first dollar.

## When to use

- "Build a business around my skills and experience"
- "What startup or side hustle should I build based on my background?"
- "Generate personalized business ideas from my unfair advantages"
- "Create an MVP roadmap and GTM plan for a business idea"
- "I want a 72-hour action plan to earn my first dollar"

## How to use

### Phase 1 — Adaptive Intake (Founder DNA Extraction)

Ask the user a structured set of discovery questions to uncover their unique founder DNA:

1. **Skills & Expertise**: What are your top 3-5 professional skills? Any technical abilities, certifications, or domain expertise?
2. **Experience & Background**: What industries have you worked in? What problems have you solved repeatedly?
3. **Unfair Advantages**: What do you have access to that most people don't? (Network, insider knowledge, geographic advantage, proprietary data, cultural fluency, existing audience)
4. **Resources & Constraints**: How much time can you dedicate weekly? What's your budget for starting? Do you have co-founders or collaborators?
5. **Preferences & Goals**: What type of business interests you? (SaaS, e-commerce, creator, hardware, services, cross-border arbitrage) What's your revenue goal in 6 months?

Adapt follow-up questions based on answers. Go deeper where the user shows unique differentiation.

### Phase 2 — Personalized Idea Generation

Generate 3-5 business ideas that are uniquely suited to the user's DNA. For each idea, provide:

- **Idea Name & One-Liner**: Clear, memorable pitch
- **Why YOU**: Explain exactly why this person is uniquely positioned to build this
- **Business Model**: How it makes money (subscription, marketplace, one-time, etc.)
- **Target Customer**: Specific persona with their pain point
- **Differentiation**: What makes this defensible vs. existing solutions
- **Difficulty Rating**: 1-5 scale for technical, marketing, and operational complexity

Cover diverse business types: SaaS, hardware, e-commerce, creator businesses, cross-border arbitrage, and services as applicable.

### Phase 3 — Deep Dive (User Selects an Idea)

Once the user picks an idea, produce a comprehensive plan:

#### Market Analysis
- Total Addressable Market (TAM) estimate
- Key competitors and their weaknesses
- Market timing assessment (why now?)
- Customer acquisition channels ranked by fit

#### Go-To-Market (GTM) Plan
- Launch strategy (pre-launch, launch day, post-launch)
- Pricing strategy with justification
- First 10 customers acquisition plan
- Content/community/outreach strategy

#### MVP Roadmap
- Core feature set (ruthlessly scoped to minimum)
- Tech stack recommendation based on user's skills
- Week-by-week build plan (4-6 weeks)
- What to explicitly NOT build yet

#### 72-Hour Action Plan
- Hour-by-hour breakdown of the first 3 days
- Specific tasks to validate demand before building
- Templates for outreach messages, landing pages, or posts
- Clear milestone: path to first dollar of revenue

### Output Format

Use clear markdown with headers, tables, and bullet points. Include actionable specifics — not generic advice. Every recommendation should tie back to the user's unique DNA.
